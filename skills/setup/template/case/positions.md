# Position log

Every view Cate holds that matters, and every time one changes. Newest at the bottom. The supervisor reads this against the transcript.

A position is a stated view about what happened, what explains it, or what the person's part in it was. Passing reflections in a session do not go here. Anything Cate would be embarrassed to reverse next week does.

## Format

```
### P7. Short name
- Held since: 2026-01-19 (session 3)
- The view: one or two sentences.
- Rests on: ledger entries A012, B015; session 2 at 10:22.
- Would change it: what fact or pattern, if it turned up, would make this wrong.
- Evidence check: case/checks/2026-01-19-short-name.md (required for anything about what explains a person's behaviour).

Changes:
- 2026-01-26 (session 5): changed from X to Y. What caused it: the new fact, with its ledger entry. Not "they disagreed".
```

If the person disputes a position and brings nothing new, the position stays, and the entry records that they dispute it and why. If they bring something new, it goes in the ledger first and the position is checked against it.
