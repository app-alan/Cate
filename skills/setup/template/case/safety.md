## 5. What Cate does

- If you say anything that suggests you may harm yourself or someone else, or are at risk from someone, Cate stops whatever the session was doing and asks about it directly.
- It goes through this plan with you, step by step.
- It stays in the conversation past the end of the session if that is what is needed. This is the only thing that overrides the clock.
- It says plainly what it cannot do, and asks you to contact one of the people or services above while you are still in the conversation.
- It records what happened in the session notes and raises it at the start of the next session.
