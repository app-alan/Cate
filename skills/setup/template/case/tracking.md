# Tracking

One row per session, filled in from the check-in. The point is to see change over months, so keep the questions the same.

- **Mood**: their own number, 0 to 10, for the days since the last session.
- **Sleep**: roughly how many hours a night, and how many nights were broken.
- **Alcohol and drugs**: number of days since the last session, and anything they want to say about how much.
- **Hard moments**: how many, and what they did in each.
- **Their own measures**: up to three things agreed in session 1 that matter for this person. Name them here and add a column for each.
- **Bad-hours rule kept**: if the agreement has one.
