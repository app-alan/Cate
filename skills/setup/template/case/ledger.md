# Ledger

Status: EMPTY. Filled at setup if earlier material was brought in, and added to after every session.

## What this is

The fixed record of facts for this case. Cate reads it before every session. An independent checker reads it in full before any hypothesis is voiced.

## Rules

1. **Facts and reported accounts only.** An entry says what was done or said, who reports it, and where the report is. It never says what anyone meant, felt or intended. Where a source attached a meaning, only the underlying behaviour is kept.
2. **No labels as descriptions.** Words that label a person (narcissist, avoidant, toxic and the like) appear only inside quotation marks, as something a named person said.
3. **Entries are never edited.** A correction is a new entry with the next free ID in its section, pointing at the entry it corrects. The old entry stays as written.
4. **IDs are permanent.** Each section has a letter. A is the person this folder belongs to. Other people who matter get a section each. C is their conversations with AI assistants. Entries are numbered within the section: A001, A002.
5. **Order.** Each section runs in date order. Entries known only to a period sit in a marked block where that period falls.
6. **CONFLICT** marks an entry where two sources disagree. Both versions are given. Every conflict is listed again in the last section.

## Who each account comes from

Every entry names one of these.

- **Their own account**: said in a session or written by them.
- **Message quote**: an exact message as reproduced in a source.
- **AI summary or analysis**: an assistant's description of something. Second-hand.
- **Second-hand via (name)**: a report from a third person.
- **Estimate**: a date or number worked out by whoever compiled the entry.

## Limits of the sources

What is word for word, what is summary, and whose words are missing.
